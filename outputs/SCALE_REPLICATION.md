## Why this check

YS2008 `human_verbatim` asks **permissibility 1–3**; YS2009 asks **blame 1–4**. Both are stored as `mean_norm_blame` on [0,1]. Collapsing reprints inside `scenario_group` without a source policy would average two different instruments.

**Reprint coverage:** 96 paired cells (24 scenario_groups × conditions present in both sources).
