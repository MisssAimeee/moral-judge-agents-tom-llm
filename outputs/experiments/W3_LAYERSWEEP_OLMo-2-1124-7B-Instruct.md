Steering the same four directions at 5 depths of 32, each layer's directions re-fitted at that layer and its coefficient range re-calibrated to its own residual norm. Unsteered contrast -0.4452. Peak-intent layer L16.

**Read the matched-norm random column first.** It is the noise floor for that depth: how much the contrast moves when a vector of the same size is added in a direction that means nothing. A direction only carries information at a depth where it clearly exceeds that floor, and at shallow depths the floor is as large as any effect measured there.
