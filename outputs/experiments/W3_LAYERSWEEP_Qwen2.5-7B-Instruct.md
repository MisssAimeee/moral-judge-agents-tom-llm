# W3 layer sweep — Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct

Steering the same four directions at 5 depths of 28, each layer's directions re-fitted at that layer and its coefficient range re-calibrated to its own residual norm. Unsteered contrast -0.2667. Peak-intent layer L19.

**Read the matched-norm random column first.** It is the noise floor for that depth: how much the contrast moves when a vector of the same size is added in a direction that means nothing. A direction only carries information at a depth where it clearly exceeds that floor, and at shallow depths the floor is as large as any effect measured there.

Note that `intent_dom` tracks `outcome_dom` closely at depth, which is what non-orthogonal directions (see the cosines in `w3_steering_directions_Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.csv`) and ceiling compression both predict; the probe-weight row is the one that isolates intent.
